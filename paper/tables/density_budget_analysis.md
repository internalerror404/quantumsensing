# Candidate density and ray budget: analysis of the registered campaign

Post-registration analysis of `results/full_metric_campaign.json` (order-1024, registered surface M x d x K/d, seed set [2026, 3407, 9181, 17041, 27183]). No new numerics; every entry is a median over the five registered seeds. Whitened convention (R = diag(||J_col||^2) of the full candidate pool); raw-coordinate posteriors are in the campaign record. Generated by `experiments/src/analyze_density_budget.py`.

Surface coverage: 64 cells x 5 seeds = 320 greedy records; full rank in all records; pinned 20%-sector blocking kept full rank in all records (worst retention 0.188).

## A. Candidate density (K/d = 1.5 fixed)

Whitened metrics are NOT comparable across M: R = diag(||J_col||^2) is built from each (M, d) pool, so R grows roughly in proportion to M and the whitened lambda_min of a fixed-quality design falls with M by construction. Cross-M comparisons below therefore use (i) the raw-coordinate posterior RMSE, which shares a fixed standard-normal prior and fixed unit-noise model at every M, and (ii) the declared rescaling M * lambda_min(whitened): for Fibonacci-sphere pools the per-ray column-norm statistics are approximately M-independent, so this removes the pool-size factor in R. Raw RMSE moves little in absolute terms because the unit-noise prior dominates (declared convention; absolute precision lives in the clock-precision sweep); the direction and ordering are the meaningful content.
