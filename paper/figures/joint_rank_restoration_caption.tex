\caption{Clock-assisted conformal rank restoration.
(A)~Ordered eigenvalues of the full-bank-whitened QFIM for $d_p=12$. Delay-only
sensing and four additional delay observations retain four numerical-zero
conformal directions, whereas four selected static-clock comparisons make the
complete spectrum positive.
(B)~Conformal nullity, common to $d_p\in\{6,8,12,16\}$, remains four after
additional delay observations, falls to one with three independent clock links,
and vanishes with four.
(C)~Mean conformal posterior standard deviation in full-bank-whitened
coordinates versus relative clock precision $\rho=\sigma_D/\sigma_R$; marked
circles denote $\rho\in\{1,10,50\}$ on both curves. The nonzero high-$\rho$
floor results from finite-delay uncertainty in physical nuisance modes entering
$R_p$, not residual conformal non-identifiability.}
